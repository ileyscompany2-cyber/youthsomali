About YPP

Rooted in community, led by youth

Peacebuilding shaped by young people and their communities.

Established on 5 February 2017, Youth for Peace and Prosperous works in Southwest State of Somalia and Mogadishu. We bring young people and communities into peacebuilding, leadership, capacity development, and inclusive initiatives.

YPP works alongside youth, women, vulnerable communities, persons with disabilities, and internally displaced people. Our approach values participation, inclusion, dialogue, and locally grounded solutions.

Our mission

Empower young people and communities through peacebuilding, leadership, capacity development, and inclusive initiatives.

Our vision

A peaceful, inclusive, and prosperous society where young people actively contribute to peacebuilding and sustainable development.

Our values

Peace and non-violence; youth leadership and participation; inclusion and equality; accountability and transparency; community ownership; and human dignity.
